faq

questions? answers.

Is it free?

Yep. Free, no ads, no subscription, no account needed.

Where does the music come from?

Your own files (mp3, flac, m4a, wav, ogg, even videos), free catalogs right in Pulse search, or your Spotify — Pulse becomes a skin for it. Pulse never downloads from YouTube or streaming services.

Windows says it's unsafe — what's up?

Windows warns about any new app that hasn't bought an expensive code-signing certificate yet. Click “More info” → “Run anyway”. Updates are signed, so the app only accepts real Pulse updates.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Any half-decent PC runs it; the 3D scenes love a real GPU, and there's a “simple” mode for older laptops.

Phone? Mac?

On the way: Android first, then iPhone, then Mac.

Where's my data?

On your PC. If you sign in with Google, a copy goes to your own Google Drive so you can move to another PC. Nothing is sold or tracked.

How do updates work?

Pulse checks for updates when it starts. One click — it updates and restarts, your music and settings stay.

Found a bug? Got an idea?

Hit the small “Feedback” button bottom-right in the app — it goes straight to the developer. Leave a contact if you want a reply.

Can I hide what I'm playing on Discord?

Sure: Settings → Discord. Show the track, only the artist, just “listening”, or turn it off.
